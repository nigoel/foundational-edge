How it works

Three steps, in order — each one earns the next.

This isn't a quiz app. It's a sequence: we find the gap, explain it clearly, then close it together, every month — and the difficulty adjusts automatically, so a Grade 3 check looks nothing like a Grade 8 one.

01
Free

Take the skill check

A short, curriculum-agnostic check calibrated to your child's grade — comparable whether they study CBSE, IB, or a national curriculum abroad. You get a score in minutes.

02
One-time

Get the diagnostic report

A specific breakdown of where the gaps are, by skill area, a 4-week improvement plan, and an optional 15-minute call with a coach trained directly by our head tutor.

03
Monthly

Track progress with a ranked check-in

Weekly practice over WhatsApp, doubt support when your child gets stuck, and a monthly ranked assessment — at the same difficulty tier as your child's grade — so you can see the movement, not guess at it.

What we test

Four skill areas, calibrated to your child's grade.

Every check adapts across three difficulty tiers from Grade 3 through Grade 8 — a Foundation-level question looks nothing like an Advanced one.

◆

Quantitative Reasoning

Numeracy, arithmetic, pre-algebra, geometry and number theory — building toward competition-style problem solving.

◆

Verbal Reasoning

Vocabulary, grammar, and reading comprehension, building to critical reading and verbal analogies at the advanced level.

◆

Logical Reasoning

Pattern recognition, classification and spatial reasoning, building to analytical deduction.

◆

Scientific Reasoning

Life, physical and applied science reasoning, aligned to school-board science curricula.

Question style and difficulty progression are informed by the leading national and international assessment formats used at each grade level.
