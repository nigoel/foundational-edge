About Swati

A tutoring method built one student at a time, now shared with every family we work with.

Swati started tutoring in 2013, working one-on-one with elementary and middle-school students who were quietly falling behind in ways their report cards never quite captured — a child who could recite multiplication tables but froze on a two-step word problem, or one who read fluently but couldn't explain what they'd just read. Over hundreds of sessions, she built a simple habit: test precisely, explain the gap in plain language, and re-check on a schedule, rather than assigning generic homework and hoping.

That habit is now the backbone of Foundational Edge. Every ranked skill check, weekly practice test, and monthly coaching call a family receives is built from the same three-part method Swati refined in her own tutoring room — and every coach on the team is trained directly by her before they work with a student.

She works with families across CBSE, ICSE, and IB curricula, and with Indian-origin families abroad who want an objective, India-benchmarked read on where their child stands — not just a local school grade that's hard to compare against anything.
